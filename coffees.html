Coffees we roasted

Washed · Colombia

Las Perlitas

Smallholder farms, Huila · 1,600–2,000 m · Pink Bourbon

Roasted May 2025 – July 2026. "Little Pearls" — smallholder lots too small to export on their own, found and bundled by The Coffee Quest's competitions in Huila. We have spent weeks in Huila and found it enchanting beyond the coffee — the monolithic head statues carved in volcanic rock.

Natural · Ethiopia

Nensebo

Nensebo, Wucheru (West Arsi) · 1,800–2,000 m · Heirloom

Roasted Nov 2024 – May 2025. Grown by Kasim, a very young farmer in Nensebo; December 2023 harvest, transparently imported by Bette Buna, who shared the farm's coordinates. We recommend this one as a pour over.

Washed · Ethiopia

Taferi Kela

Taferi Kela, Sidama · 2,100 m · 74112 & Enat Buna D'ara Otilicho

Roasted Sep 2024 – Jan 2025. From Bette Buna's Taferi Kela farm, December 2023 harvest. A classic Ethiopian Sidama washed cup with great balance and notes of marzipan, bergamot and rooibos.

Extended natural · Ethiopia

Megadu

Megadu, Guji · 1,800–2,200 m · 74112, 74110 & Enat Buna Megadu

Roasted July – Sep 2024. Cherries from Dawit & Hester's farm, dried for eight weeks in shade with careful moisture management. We brew it with a higher water-to-coffee ratio — 360 g water to 20 g coffee — and the last of the extraction stays sweet and smooth.

Washed · Kenya

Gloria

Sakami, Trans-Nzoia County · 1,800 m · Ruiru 11, Batian, SL28, K7

Roasted Mar – July 2024. Gloria gave up her career as a beauty expert to dedicate her life to coffee on Sakami — the estate she and her husband Jarmo named after their daughters. Complex, with flavours that evolve in the mouth. Best 1–4 weeks after roast date.

Anoxic natural · Costa Rica

Don Mayo

La Ladera, Tarrazú · 1,850 m · Catuai

Roasted Nov 2023 – Mar 2024. Washed cherries sealed airtight and fermented for 36 hours, then slow-dried for eight days plus patios at Don Mayo micromill. Best 2–4 weeks after roast date. Fun fact: anoxic is the correct term for what many call anaerobic — fermentation is always anaerobic, but the oxygen-free environment is anoxic. It also fits better on the label.
