Currently roasting

Washed · Kenya

Mukunga

Roasting since July 2026

Origin Mukunga Estate, Kiambu County, Kenya
Altitude 1,850 m
Varietal Ruiru & SL28
Process Washed

The estate lies between 1,800 and 1,850 meters above sea level, in the coffee–tea transition zone. Soils are red volcanic and well-drained, with reliable bimodal rainfall. The estate was started in 1970 — around 90% of the trees are older Ruiru varieties, 10% newer SL28 plantings.

Cherries are selectively handpicked, de-pulped, then dry-fermented for 24 hours in open tanks before being washed and submerged in clean water for another 24 hours. The wet parchment dries on raised beds for 10 to 18 days, depending on the weather.

How we roast

We roast one coffee at a time. Coffee roasting is a hobby that fits into life — among work, family and music. Solving the roast mystery one bean at a time is the chosen pace.

Each coffee is tasted and pondered every day for months, and the roast profile adapts. The longer we have the coffee, the more completely its flavour is communicated through the roast.

The paintings alongside our coffees are taste impressions — little paintings made as an effort to describe the flavours we perceive in the coffee, where words muddle us.

Roasted Mar – July 2024. Gloria gave up her career as a beauty expert to dedicate her life to coffee on Sakami — the estate she and her husband Jarmo named after their daughters. Complex, with flavours that evolve in the mouth. Best 1–4 weeks after roast date.

Anoxic natural · Costa Rica

Don Mayo

La Ladera, Tarrazú · 1,850 m · Catuai

Roasted Nov 2023 – Mar 2024. Washed cherries sealed airtight and fermented for 36 hours, then slow-dried for eight days plus patios at Don Mayo micromill. Best 2–4 weeks after roast date. Fun fact: anoxic is the correct term for what many call anaerobic — fermentation is always anaerobic, but the oxygen-free environment is anoxic. It also fits better on the label.
