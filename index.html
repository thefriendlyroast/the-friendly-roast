What we're about

We're not a big operation — and that's the point. Every batch is roasted by hand, watched closely, and tasted together. Our quest is simple: to share compelling coffee with our friends.

From 250 grams to a full kilo

We started in 2021 on a modified Gene Cafe, roasting tiny 250 g batches to learn the craft. Today we roast on an Aillio Bullet — still in small batches, still learning something new every roast.
