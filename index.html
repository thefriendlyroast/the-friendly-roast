Michael roasts one coffee at a time

Washed · Kenya

Mukunga

Roasting since July 2026

Origin Mukunga Estate, Kiambu County, Kenya
Altitude 1,850 m
Varietal Ruiru & SL28
Process Washed

The estate lies between 1,800 and 1,850 meters above sea level, in the coffee–tea transition zone. Soils are red volcanic and well-drained, with reliable bimodal rainfall. The estate was started in 1970 — around 90% of the trees are older Ruiru varieties, 10% newer SL28 plantings.

Cherries are selectively handpicked, de-pulped, then dry-fermented for 24 hours in open tanks before being washed and submerged in clean water for another 24 hours. The wet parchment dries on raised beds for 10 to 18 days, depending on the weather.

Coffee roasting is a hobby that fits into life — among work, family, music, life. Solving the roast mystery one coffee at a time is the chosen pace.

That coffee is tasted and pondered every day for months. The roast profile adapts. The longer the coffee is in his possession, the more completely its flavour is communicated through the roast profile.
